# Historical Local Language Bank

The supplied bank and connector scaffold were retired from active composition
under [D-060](../governance/DECISIONS.md#d-060-adapt-the-v10-foundation-to-hughs-colour-flow).
The `language-bank` command, loader and packaged resource are removed. The
[composer guide](COMPOSITION.md) owns the bank-free runtime.

## Preserved Source

The final bank was `0.5.1`: 105 language entries, including 15 authorial references
and 90 assistant candidates, plus 14 connector senses. Source wording, meanings,
conditions and version history remain available at the pre-removal revision:

- [Bank resource](https://github.com/tryskian/huemiliator/blob/aee2ebc20d367a152066c588d7a36ff7e71bf07b/src/huemiliator/data/language_bank.json)
- [Complete historical guide](https://github.com/tryskian/huemiliator/blob/aee2ebc20d367a152066c588d7a36ff7e71bf07b/docs/runtime/LANGUAGE_BANK.md)
- [Library research and original observations](../research/450_LOCAL_LANGUAGE.md)
- [Construction failures and pending cue mini](../research/220_RESPONSE_CONSTRUCTION.md)

Historical requests retain the snapshots they actually supplied. Their original
responses, mechanical results and attributed judgments remain unchanged.

## Conditions and Connector Meaning

The [historical conditions and connector guide](https://github.com/tryskian/huemiliator/blob/aee2ebc20d367a152066c588d7a36ff7e71bf07b/docs/runtime/LANGUAGE_BANK.md#conditions-and-connector-meaning)
preserves the earlier annotation contract. These records explain the experiment;
Hugh now develops connections and sentence construction from the adapted directions
and colour facts. They impose no active vocabulary or sentence template.
